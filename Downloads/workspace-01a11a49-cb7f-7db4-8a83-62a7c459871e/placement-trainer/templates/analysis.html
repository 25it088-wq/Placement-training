How this is calculated

  • Smoothed accuracy: (correct + 1) / (answered + 2) per category.
  • Status: strong at 70%+ with no below-peer gap; weak at 45% or lower, or a peer z-score of -1 or below; otherwise developing.
  • Peer z-score: how far you sit from the other students in the same category.
  • Learning profile: KMeans clustering of all students' category accuracy vectors.
  • Trend and prediction: linear regression over your test scores (needs at least 3 tests).
